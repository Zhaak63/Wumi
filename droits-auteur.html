05Reporting content

If content shared through Wumi or wumi.fr infringes your rights, write to zhaakpro@gmail.com with:

  1. your name and a way to reach you;
  2. the work concerned and proof of your rights;
  3. exactly where the content is (link, server, message);
  4. a statement that the information is accurate.

We reply within 7 days and remove content that is clearly unlawful. For content posted by a member on Discord, also report it to Discord.
