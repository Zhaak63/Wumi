Ready in three steps

  1. Add Wumi to your server with the button above.
  2. Run /setup, or open the dashboard and click through the settings.
  3. Type / in Discord and try /quiz play with your members!

What matters

  • No Administrator permission. The invite link asks only for what Wumi needs: roles, moderation and messages.
  • English and French. Commands and messages follow your server's language.
  • Your data stays yours. Remove Wumi and everything about your server is erased. No copy is kept.
  • Always online, and you can check it. Live status and 90 days of uptime on wumi.fr/statut.
