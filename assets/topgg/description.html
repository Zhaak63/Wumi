All-in-one Discord bot

Wumi is a cute all-in-one bot for your Discord server. Music and music games, levels and rank cards, welcome images, tickets, moderation, live alerts and collectible cards, all set up with slash commands or a full web dashboard, in English or French.

Get started

Ready in three steps.

  1. Add Wumi to your server with the button above.
  2. Run /setup, or open the dashboard and pick your modules.
  3. Type / in Discord and try /rank or /musicgame quiz with your members!
Good to know
  • No Administrator permission. The invite link asks only for what Wumi needs: roles, moderation and messages.
  • English and French. Commands and messages follow your server's language.
  • Your data stays yours. Remove Wumi and everything about your server is erased.
  • Always online, and you can check it. Live status and 90 days of uptime on wumi.fr/statut.
  • Clear rules. Read the Terms, the Privacy Policy and the Refund policy.
