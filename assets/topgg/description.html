Wumi is a cute all-in-one Discord bot. It welcomes newcomers, hands out roles, helps you moderate and adds a bit of fun, with simple slash commands and no headaches.

What Wumi does for you

Simple tools to manage your community.

🎀

Auto roles

Give one or more roles to every new member, with separate settings for humans and bots.

🌸

Reaction roles

Members pick their own roles with a button or an emoji, right inside a message.

👋

Welcome and goodbye

Custom greeting and farewell messages with the member's name, avatar and the member count.

🛡️

Moderation

Ban, kick, timeout and clear a channel. Every action can be kept in a logs channel.

🎮

Games and fun

Mini-games and silly commands to keep your server lively.

⚡

Slash commands

Type / in Discord and pick a command. Nothing to memorize.

See it in action

The Wumi website, in English and French.

One command to set an auto role, and Wumi welcomes every new member.

Ready in three steps

  1. Add Wumi to your server with the button above.
  2. Run /autorole and pick the role new members should get.
  3. Wumi welcomes the next member and gives them their role.

What matters

  • No Administrator permission. The invite link asks only for what Wumi needs: roles, moderation and messages.
  • Your data stays yours. Remove Wumi and everything about your server is erased immediately. No copy is kept.
  • Free. Wumi is a free bot. Read the Terms and the Privacy Policy.
