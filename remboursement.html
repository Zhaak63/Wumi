In short: Premium is a Discord subscription, paid and refunded through Discord. You can stop it whenever you want. If Wumi doesn't deliver, write to us: we extend your Premium for free.

01What you buy

Wumi Premium is a per-server subscription, at the price shown on the Premium page and in Discord at checkout (taxes depend on your country). It unlocks the Premium features on the chosen server while the subscription is active.

02Who takes the payment

You buy it inside Discord. Discord Inc. takes the payment, issues the receipt and applies its Paid Services Terms. Wumi never sees your card or payment account.

03Stopping the subscription

  1. Open Discord › User Settings › Subscriptions.
  2. Pick the Wumi subscription and cancel it.
  3. Premium stays active until the end of the period already paid, then stops. Nothing more is charged.

04Asking for a refund

Refunds are decided and paid by Discord, under its terms: ask at support.discord.com and say it is an app subscription. Wumi does not hold the money and cannot refund directly.

Right of withdrawal (EU): Premium is digital content supplied immediately. Under EU consumer law, the 14-day withdrawal period no longer applies once you have agreed, at checkout, that the service starts right away. Discord may still grant a refund under its own rules.

05When Wumi owes you

Even though we can't refund ourselves, we take responsibility:

  • Long outage (bot down for more than 72 hours in a row, see the status page): the length of the outage is given back as Premium, and more if it's fair.
  • Broken Premium feature we don't fix within 7 days of your report: we extend your Premium by that long.
  • Premium not activated after payment: we turn it on by hand as soon as we have your Discord receipt.

Write to us with the server name and, if possible, the purchase date.

06Gifted Premium and trials

Gifted Premium (giveaways, thank-yous, free trials) is not refundable since it cost nothing. It can be removed if the server breaks the terms of service.

07If Wumi shuts down

If the bot ever closed, Premium sales would stop and it would be announced on the support server at least 30 days before. For the period already paid, the refund request goes to Discord, and we will back you up.
