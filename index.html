A FEW ANSWERS

Before you get started.

Another question?
Is Wumi free?

Yes. The bot and all the Aesthetic Creators tools are free.

How do I set up the bot?

Everything happens with slash commands in Discord. Type / and pick a Wumi command, or use /aide to see the list.

Do the tools need the bot?

No. The tools run in your browser, with or without Wumi on your server.

Why isn't Wumi giving a role?

Check that Wumi's role sits above the role to give in your server settings, and that it has the Manage Roles permission.

How do I join the community?

Join the support server to follow the project, ask questions and share feedback.
