Set up with one command

Pick a role and Wumi handles the rest. Every new member is welcomed and equipped the moment they arrive.

M
Maya /autorole add role: @Member
WumiBOT
All set! New arrivals will automatically receive @Member.
WumiBOT
Welcome to the server, @Noa! I gave you the @Member role :3

What Wumi does for you

Simple tools to manage your community, no headaches.

🎀

Auto roles

Give one or more roles to every new member, with separate settings for humans and bots.

🌸

Reaction roles

Members pick their own roles by clicking a button or an emoji, right inside a message.

👋

Welcome and goodbye

Greeting and farewell messages you customize with the member’s name, avatar and the member count.

🛡️

Moderation

Ban, kick, timeout, clear a channel. Every action can be kept in a logs channel.

🎮

Games and fun

Mini-games and silly commands to keep your server lively.

The essential commands

Wumi uses slash commands: type / in Discord and pick one.

/autorole/reactionrole/welcome/ban/kick/timeout/clear/logs/serverinfo/userinfo/tictactoe/help
